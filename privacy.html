Privacy Policy

Application: ARCH Reference AI

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Purpose

ARCH Reference AI is a personal architectural research application designed to organize and analyze architectural design references from Pinterest.

2. Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access Pinterest boards, saved Pins, image references, descriptions, and related metadata through the Pinterest API.

3. Use of Information

Information is used to organize architectural references, search saved design examples, and classify architectural characteristics such as building massing, facade materials, courtyards, and landscape integration.

4. Storage and Security

Authorized Pinterest data may be processed and stored for personal research. Reasonable measures will be taken to protect access credentials and stored information.

5. Sharing

The application is intended for personal use. Pinterest account information is not sold. Any use of external AI or hosting services will be disclosed here before such processing is enabled.

6. Data Deletion

The account owner may revoke Pinterest authorization and request deletion of information stored by the application.

7. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner through the GitHub profile: github.com/jdjangs.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated as the application develops.
